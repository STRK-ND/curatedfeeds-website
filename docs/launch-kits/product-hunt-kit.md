## Description (the "What is it?" field)

```
Curated Feeds is a free RSS reader for Android built for focused reading. Articles
arrive as cards — swipe right to save a story, swipe left to mark it done, and the
queue stays one card deep, so there is never a backlog to manage.

- Reads offline: every article you open is cached on the phone
- No account: feeds, saved articles, and history stay on your device
- No ads, no tracking, no premium tier
- Any RSS 2.0, Atom, or RDF feed: paste the URL, or start with 8 built-in feeds
  (TechCrunch, Hacker News, The Verge, Ars Technica, and more)

Built in Flutter. Free forever. ~67 MB, Android 8.0+.
```

## Maker comment (post first, as the maker — this sets the tone)

```
Hi PH — I'm Rajat, sole builder of Curated Feeds.

The origin was a measurement, not an idea: in 2022 I tracked 3h15m a day lost to
algorithmic feeds — 1,186 hours a year. I swapped social apps for RSS, and the habit
stuck, but every reader I tried turned triage into a filing session: stars, folders,
tags, an unread count that only went up.

So I built the opposite constraint: the queue is one card deep. One story on screen,
swipe right to keep it, swipe left to let it go. Everything else followed from that:
no account (nothing to sync means nothing to surveil), full offline caching, and no
ads — there's no server bill to fund, so there's no premium tier.

The honest trade-offs: no iOS yet, no cross-device sync, no web reader. If you read
on one Android phone and want your reading to stay yours, this is built for you.

Happy to answer build questions — Flutter, the offline cache, the Works-based APK
delivery, all fair game.
```
