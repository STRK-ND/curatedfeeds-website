## Full description

```
Curated Feeds is a free RSS reader for Android built for focused, distraction-free
reading. Articles arrive as cards: swipe right to save a story for later, swipe left
to mark it done. The queue stays one card deep, so there is never a backlog to manage.

Key features:
• Card-based triage — one article at a time, decided with a swipe
• Offline reading — every opened article is cached on the device
• No account — feeds, saved articles, and reading history stay on your phone
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no premium tier
• Unlimited feeds — any RSS 2.0, Atom, or RDF URL; 8 starter feeds included
• Categories — group sources by topic (Tech, Design, News, AI, …)
• Dark theme tuned for night reading

Built in Flutter. Distributed as a direct APK (~67 MB, Android 8.0+) and on Uptodown.
There is no server sync: the trade-off for no account and no tracking is that reading
state stays on one device.
```
