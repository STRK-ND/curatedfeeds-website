## Long description (≤1000 chars)
Curated Feeds is a free, privacy-first RSS reader for Android, built by Rajat Kashyap. It replaces the endless scroll with a deck of cards: read an article, swipe right to save it for later or left to mark it done, and the feed stays short and intentional. Add unlimited RSS 2.0, Atom and RDF feeds from tech blogs, news sites and podcasts; organize them into categories; and read offline because articles cache on first read. A dark indigo theme is tuned for night reading. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no account — your feeds, saved articles and reading history stay on your device, and only anonymous aggregate counters are recorded. Written in Flutter and Dart. Distributed as a direct APK (~67 MB) and on Uptodown. Free forever. Website: https://curatedfeeds.web.app. Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/curated_feeds.
