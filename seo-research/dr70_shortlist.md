**Date checked:** 2026-10-29
**Target:** https://curatedfeeds.web.app

## Result

No opportunity was verified as **both** (a) Domain Rating 70+ and (b) submittable without creating an account/login.

This is an honest finding, not a gap in research. Across general directories, software directories, RSS/feed directories, app stores, launchpads, and community sites surveyed, every high-authority (DR 70+) property that accepts product submissions requires either:

- an account / SSO / magic-link login (Product Hunt, AlternativeTo, SaaSHub priority, SourceForge, Crunchbase, G2, Trustpilot, Clutch, Devpost, Built In, GeekWire, YourStory, FinancesOnline, TrustRadius, Hacker News, DEV Community, Hashnode, Indie Hackers, StackShare, Open Collective, Lobsters, GitHub awesome lists, Feedspot, Uptodown developer console), or
- editorial outreach (Zapier, PCMag, TechRadar, Tom's Guide, etc.), or
- a reciprocal link / badge (I Heart RSS, Fazier free tier, OnTopList free review).

The account-free submissions that do exist (rss.mobi, rssamplifier.com, DevElif, Directory Index, Zearches, MeWebLink, weblist.dev, AppRater, AppLauncher, ListYourApp, StarterLaunch, GreenRocket, 1000.tools, SaaSHub free form, ooh.directory, feedle, etc.) are low-authority (DR not verified, estimated well below 70) or unverified.
