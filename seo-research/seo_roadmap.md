# Deliverable G — 30-day SEO roadmap

## Week 1 — Foundation & verification (Days 1-7)
- Day 1: Verify all site pages indexable; submit sitemap to Google Search Console (manual; requires GSC access).
- Day 2: Confirm robots.txt/sitemap live; check canonical tags on all pages.
- Day 3: Verify structured data (SoftwareApplication, Organization, Article) via Rich Results Test.
- Day 4: Decide authorized contact email + approve submission policy.
- Day 5: Execute P2 batch 1 (rss.mobi, rssamplifier.com, saashub.com, 1000.tools) — no-account submissions.
- Day 6: Execute P2 batch 2 (DevElif, Directory Index, Zearches, MeWebLink, weblist.dev, AppLauncher, ListYourApp, StarterLaunch).
- Day 7: Record outcomes in submission_log.csv; verify published listings.

## Week 2 — Content & outreach prep (Days 8-14)
- Day 8: Draft "RSS reader for Android" landing page.
- Day 9: Draft comparison hub pages (vs Feedly, Inoreader, NetNewsWire, FeedFlow).
- Day 10: Expand /sources/ into "100 best RSS feeds" resource.
- Day 11: Build outreach list for P3 (Zapier, PCMag, TechRadar, Tom's Guide, etc.).
- Day 12: Write outreach emails (personalized, one ask per email).
- Day 13: Send first 5 outreach emails.
- Day 14: Create/verify social profiles (GitHub, LinkedIn) linking to site.

## Week 3 — Account-required queue (Days 15-21)
- Day 15: Founder creates accounts for Product Hunt, AlternativeTo, Hacker News, DEV, Hashnode.
- Day 16: Submit to AlternativeTo, SaaSHub (already), SourceForge.
- Day 17: Prepare Product Hunt launch assets (screenshots, tagline, first comment).
- Day 18: Show HN post (follow rules; working product, not landing page).
- Day 19: Publish DEV.to / Hashnode article (cross-post blog).
- Day 20: Submit to Indie Hackers, StackShare.
- Day 21: Review all submissions; update log.

## Week 4 — Measurement & iteration (Days 22-30)
- Day 22: Check indexing of published listings.
- Day 23: Verify backlinks (dofollow/nofollow) via manual check.
- Day 24: Analyze referral traffic (Firebase Analytics).
- Day 25: Outreach follow-ups (1 week after first email).
- Day 26: Publish one new blog post (long-tail keyword target).
- Day 27: Internal linking pass across all pages.
- Day 28: Review keyword rankings (manual SERP check for brand + 5 core terms).
- Day 29: Update keyword_database with any new findings.
- Day 30: Compile monthly report; plan next cycle.

## KPIs
- Submissions completed (target: 15+ no-account by day 7)
- Live backlinks verified (target: 10+)
- Indexed pages (target: all sitemap URLs indexed)
- Referral traffic from directories (target: measurable)
- Keyword rankings for brand + 3 core terms (target: top 20)
- Outreach replies (target: 2+)

## Dependencies
- Founder authorization for email use and account creation.
- GSC access for measurement.
- Time/effort: ~2-3 hours/day.
